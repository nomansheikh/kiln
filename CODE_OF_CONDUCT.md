# Code of Conduct

Kiln adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link is the code of conduct for this project; this page is a summary and tells you how to report a problem.

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project, as a contributor or a maintainer, agrees to make participation free of harassment for everyone, whatever their background, identity, appearance or level of experience. We aim to act in ways that keep the community open, welcoming and healthy.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions and experiences.
- Give feedback constructively and accept it gracefully.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, reviews and any other channel the project uses) and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer through https://github.com/nomansheikh.

For anything sensitive that you do not want to raise in public, use the repository's private reporting form: [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/nomansheikh/kiln/security/advisories/new) under the Security tab. It reaches only the maintainer.

Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and will follow the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) when deciding on consequences.
